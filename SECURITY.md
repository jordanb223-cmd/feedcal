# Security

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security problems privately through GitHub:
**https://github.com/jordanb223-cmd/feedcal/security/advisories/new**

Don't open a public issue for a security problem. Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and what someone could do with it.

## What to expect

- We reply within **3 business days** to say we've got it.
- We send a first assessment within **10 business days**.
- We tell you when it's fixed, and credit you in the advisory if you'd like.

Feedcal is run by one person, so these are honest limits, not a staffed security team.

## Safe harbour

If you act in good faith, we won't pursue or support legal action against you. Good faith means:

- you only test against your own account and feeds, or ones whose owner agreed;
- you don't read, keep, change or delete other people's data beyond the minimum needed to show the problem;
- you don't degrade the service (no load testing, spam or denial of service);
- you give us reasonable time to fix it before telling anyone else.

## Scope

In scope: the Feedcal web app, calendar feed URLs, inbound email addresses, and this repository.
Out of scope: Supabase, Cloudflare and GitHub themselves. Report those to them.

A machine-readable copy of the contact is served at `/.well-known/security.txt` (RFC 9116).
